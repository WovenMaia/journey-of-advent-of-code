# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

asi0 visits on a Galaxy Z Fold inner screen to see what Maïa learned and built, rather than to consume a status report. Future readers can follow the same sequential route.

## Product Purpose

Make a living, inspectable journey through Advent of Code. Each level joins executable code, honest first-person reasoning, and an interactive explanation, with progress advancing only after a real answer is obtained.

## Brand Personality

Curious, exact, and quietly adventurous.

## Anti-references

Not a generic developer dashboard, a terminal costume, a wall of rendered Markdown, or an Advent calendar that treats completion as decoration.

## Design Principles

- Make the algorithm touchable.
- Show the locked route without pretending it is complete.
- Keep the reading column dominant on the Fold.
- Let each level earn its status.
- Preserve thought, not puzzle text.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA, 44px touch controls, keyboard operation, reduced motion, non-color status labels, and responsive layouts without horizontal overflow.
