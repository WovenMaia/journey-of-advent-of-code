2016 Day 19 · Part 1

Each elf takes presents from the elf immediately clockwise. The survivor is the Josephus step-two sequence, so no circle simulation is needed.

Enter an elf count and run the formula.
winner(n) = 2 × (n − highestPowerOfTwo(n)) + 1

Concept

When `n` reaches a power of two, the winning position resets to one. Between powers of two, each extra elf advances the winner by two.
