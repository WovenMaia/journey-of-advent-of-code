Dragon checksum

2016 · Day 16 · Part 1

Expand the binary data with the dragon rule until the disk is full, then reduce equal pairs to 1 and unequal pairs to 0.

This reader demo uses the published example's 20-bit disk. The full real-input disk is solved by the Python program.

Try the example

Checksum: 01100

My answer

10111110010110110, computed locally from the ignored input.

Idea

Each expansion appends a zero and the reversed, inverted data. Pairwise reduction halves the string until its length is odd.
