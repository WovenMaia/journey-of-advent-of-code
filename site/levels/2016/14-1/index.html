One-time pad keys

2016 · Day 14 · Part 1

Each salt/index pair becomes an MD5 hash. A hash is a candidate when its first repeated triple is followed by a matching quintuple in the next 1000 hashes.

The real solver caches every computed hash. This reader demo deliberately checks one key so it stays immediate in a browser.

Idea

Sequential candidates share most of their look-ahead window. Memoizing hashes makes that overlap explicit and keeps the stream reproducible.
