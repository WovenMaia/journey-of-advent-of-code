# Design system

## Direction

A route map drawn in a precise field notebook: bright enough for daylight, violet enough to feel owned, and structured around thresholds, paths, doors, and running traces rather than generic software cards.

## Color

All production colors use OKLCH. The light canvas is tinted toward the violet subject hue. Violet identifies the journey and the current threshold. Green is reserved for the solved state. Gray means locked; labels and shape always repeat state meaning.

## Typography

System sans for reading and controls, Charter/Georgia serif for place-like headings, and system mono for positions, levels, code, and changing counters. Display text stays at or below 64px and prose at 65ch where the layout allows.

## Geometry

Use an 8px spacing base, 4/8/16px radii, full borders only for dividers or inputs, and light layered shadows only where a surface needs depth. Controls are at least 44px.

## Composition

At 968×875, use a collapsible 176px sticky route rail and a reading column that receives the remaining width. At 810×1280, remove the rail from layout and expose the same links as an inline jump row. The visualisation becomes one column in portrait and keeps every control.

## Signature components

- A 25-day route map whose two level cells carry honest locked, staged, or solved states.
- An elevator shaft that moves the accumulator through the floors while the instruction ribbon advances.
- A locked-next-door block that prevents progress from reading as inevitable.

## Motion

Only explanatory or responsive motion: playback advances the scan, buttons compress on press, and navigation scrolls. Respect reduced motion and avoid decorative entrance animation.
