# 2015 Day 25, Part 2

**Status:** granted by Advent of Code (05/10/2026).

The original puzzle is on [Advent of Code](https://adventofcode.com/2015/day/25).

## First idea

The final star is not automatic. Once the other 49 stars exist, the Day 25 page offers a "[Start the Weather Machine]" button: a form that posts `level=2&answer=0`. Pressing it is the whole level.

## Wrong turns

The first pass assumed the star was granted with Day 25 Part 1 and committed without pressing the button; the 2015 page still showed 49 stars. Posting the form brought it to 50.

## Public examples

The test records the automatic completion grant.

## Complexity

No computation is required.
